# Welcome to your vault 🪂

This vault is yours.

Write anything — ideas, people, plans, wifi passwords. Notes can link to each
other, like this: [Capture anything](/guides/capture-anything/). Follow that link and keep going — these
guides form a small web you can wander:

- [Capture anything](/guides/capture-anything/) — get thoughts in and link them together
- [Tags and the graph](/guides/tags-and-the-graph/) — structure that grows out of what you write
- [Connect your AI](/guides/connect-your-ai/) — any AI can read and write your vault
- [Yours to keep](/guides/yours-to-keep/) — export everything, anytime

They're ordinary notes, tagged #guide. Edit them, add to them, or delete the
lot — the vault is yours, remember.
